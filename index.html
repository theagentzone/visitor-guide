The sessions

What you'll see and take away

Everyone joins the keynote. At 2:00 PM, the room splits into two breakouts, one for IT and one for business leaders.

Keynote · 12:15 – 1:45 PM

Microsoft AI Vision: From Copilot to Agentic AI

AI is moving from answering questions to finishing work. See what that means for your organization, and how to adopt it responsibly.

  • See one workflow at every level of delegation: ask, delegate, build, and ongoing coordination
  • Understand the new Copilot experience: Home, Code, and Autopilot
  • Learn when to ask, delegate, build, or hand work to an always-on teammate
  • See where trust, governance, and human judgment fit, and what it costs

Leave with: a clear view of the path from assistance to delegated work, anchored in trusted context

IT breakout · 2:00 – 3:00 PM · Room 1

Demos and roadmap discussions

See the demos, the roadmap, and the controls behind secure, scalable AI adoption.

  • From Copilot assistant to delegation with Cowork: a bounded task becomes a finished, editable deliverable, with admins and reviewers in the loop
  • Democratizing solution building with Copilot Code: build purpose-built solutions, and know when to choose Code or a custom business agent (preview)
  • Your digital teammate, Autopilot: persistent work changes identity, boundaries, and monitoring needs (preview)
  • Manage AI spend with confidence: a consumption-and-value model with limits and outcome measures
  • The control plane for AI agents, Agent 365: observe, govern, and secure agents with human oversight

Leave with: your prerequisites, a first bounded deployment, and a control model

Business breakout · 2:00 – 3:00 PM · Room 2

Workflows, outcomes, adoption

Turn AI interest into measurable outcomes: pick the right workflow, prove the value, keep it accountable.

  • Workflows: choose a meaningful initiative before choosing features
  • Measurable outcomes with Copilot: measure useful work completed, not prompts submitted
  • Cowork: a complete, reviewed deliverable, such as a client briefing, with human approval
  • Agent sprawl control (Studio and Agent 365): keep business agents visible, owned, and within bounds
  • FinOps: what improved, at what quality, at what cost

Leave with: one workflow, business and IT owners, and one outcome measure
